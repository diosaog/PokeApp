# Liga Nocturna — art pass 2: scene backdrops

2026-10-27. Entry `40bc2f7` (Liga Nocturna DONE LOCAL). The owner approved
Liga Nocturna but found the backdrops plain; this pass gives every area its own
original scene, with Home the most spectacular, and keeps competitive screens
calm. Design reference: [design-system.md](design-system.md#art-pass-2--scenes).

## Direction

Backdrops read as **game interface scenes**, not landscapes: a night stadium
with its scoreboard, a market counter, a tactical scope, championship banners,
a trainer licence. Modern Pokémon (Galar stadium energy) first, DS/3DS echoes
(dual-screen box, Pokédex-like scope) as accents. Presence is subtle-to-medium:
each scene fades towards the reading side and never sits behind a title.

## Scenes (all original, generated)

`web/scripts/build-scenes.mjs` draws every asset from geometry and a seeded
random sequence: reproducible byte for byte, reviewable, no third-party or
traced images. Output `web/src/design/scenes/` (12 SVG, 99 KB; each loaded only
on its screen, the four smallest inlined).

| Area | Scene | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inicio | Night stadium: floodlight masts, jumbotron with two ball emblems, tiered stands with a crowd, striped pitch with the ball in the centre circle | Beams sweep from the masts, camera flashes in the stands, sky drift, rising sparkles (strongest motion of the app) |
| Tienda | Warm market: pennant garland, three hanging lamps, two shelving units with balls, sprays, berries and gift boxes | Lamps breathe out of step; sparkles |
| Scouting | Tactical scope over a hex field: rings, ticks, crosshair, lock-on brackets, data call-outs | Slow radar sweep; holographic glint on the selected rival |
| Liga | Hanging banners, trophy in laurel branches, podium, golden rays | Rays turn very slowly; sparkles |
| Perfil | Trainer licence guilloché (rosettes, micro-lines, badge case) — also printed inside the licence card | Static |
| Hall | Spotlight and laurel wreath with gold stars | Spotlight sways; sparkles |
| Mi PC / Saves | Dual-screen handheld: a box with its title tab and arrows above, the party below, d-pad and buttons | Static (box arrows untouched) |
| Batallas | Calm versus split | Static |
| Copa | Bracket rising to a crown | Static |
| Juicios / Admin | Quiet blueprint grid | Static |
| All | Night base (deep gradient, auroras) and a drifting star field | Sky drift |

Home: the stadium is centred on the content (not the window, which includes the
sidebar) and scales with it, so both floodlights stay in view from the Launcher's
default window (1104 × 755) to 1920 px; a title band above the hero shows the
scoreboard, and the hero becomes glass over the stands.

Phones: Tienda keeps only the garland and lamps as a top band; Liga moves the
trophy to the right edge; all scenes are lighter and fade sooner.

Launcher: its native panels use the same night base and star field (a block
in `companion/launcher/style.css` generated by the same script, data URI, so
the package gains no file).

## Problems found and fixed during review

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Stadium off-centre (centred on the window, not the content) and floodlights cut in the Launcher window | Positioned and sized from the content area (`--side-w`, `--stage-w`) |
| Scoreboard hidden behind the hero | Title band above the hero (96 px desktop, 72 px phone) |
| Shelves behind the shop title on phones; trophy behind the league title on phones | Phone-only masks and positions |
| PC scene read as a flat table | Redrawn as a dual-screen handheld |
| Copa bracket too strong for a secondary area | Opacity lowered |
| Layout shift 0 → 0.168 when moving between areas (the per-area light reused one pseudo-element and changed its geometry) | Lights live on a decorative node React recreates per area: CLS 0 again |

- Sounds and preferences are unchanged from Liga Nocturna.
